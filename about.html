Who We Are

Our team is a dynamic and collaborative group of trainees, drawing on a diverse range of knowledge and expertise across various disciplines. Our members include PhD students and enthusiastic newcomers eager to engage with the scientific community. We recognize that each individual's perspective and experience are crucial for driving successful interdisciplinary research and achieving significant scientific advancements. The unique insights each person brings enrich our collective understanding and enhance our research efforts. To foster this spirit of collaboration, we prioritize building a strong sense of community and creating an engaging learning environment. We cultivate a culture of mutual respect, ensuring that every team member feels valued and heard. Recognizing the importance of social connections, we actively promote interactions that strengthen our community. Mentorship is a cornerstone of our approach, as we believe that supporting and guiding one another is essential for both personal and professional growth. By embracing these principles, we empower everyone to thrive and contribute meaningfully to our research initiatives. Together, we are committed to advancing science and innovation through collaboration, respect, and shared learning.

Our Approach

Sustainable Biomaterials

We explore renewable and thoughtfully sourced polymers to design biomaterials that minimize environmental impact while meeting rigorous performance demands in the clinic.

Translational Mindset

Our projects are guided by real clinical questions. We work alongside clinicians and collaborators to ensure our materials have a clear path toward therapeutic use.

Interdisciplinary Design

We sit at the intersection of chemistry, materials science, and biomedical engineering, allowing us to design systems that integrate mechanical, chemical, and biological function.

Life in the Lab

Where People and Science Grow Together

Our team is a dynamic community of trainees—ranging from new researchers to advanced PhD students—who bring diverse experiences and perspectives. We know that the best science happens when everyone feels respected, supported, and heard.

We intentionally cultivate:

  • Collaboration: Sharing ideas, troubleshooting together, and celebrating wins as a group.
  • Mentorship: Supporting each person’s growth through guidance, feedback, and skill-building.
  • Community: Creating an environment where people enjoy coming to the lab and learning from one another.

Whether you are joining us as a trainee, collaborator, or visitor, our goal is to provide a welcoming, engaging environment where you can contribute meaningfully and grow as a scientist.
