About Our Lab

Our Mission

The goal of our research group is to address healthcare challenges by employing chemistry strategies to develop materials that are sustainable, scalable, and clinically relevant. Our materials approach combines the molecular design creativity of chemists with the application-oriented mindset of biomaterial scientists to create comprehensive solutions and innovations in biomaterials design, drug delivery, and regenerative medicine. Through the use of sustainably sourced polymers that are cost-effective and readily accessible, our goal is to expand the use of these materials for human health applications in a manner that is environmentally and economically responsible. These user-friendly materials will be designed as 1. Catheter-injectable gels for drug delivery and tissue engineering, 2. 3D-printing bioinks, and 3. In vitro disease modeling. In each of these thrusts, we will use a holistic approach that combines our technical expertise and scientific creativity in materials development with a thorough understanding of the unmet medical need. Through this, we aim to develop the next generation of healthcare therapies to advance sustainable engineering practices and impact patient health.

Research Areas

Catheter-Injectable Gel for Drug Delivery to the Beating Heart

Developing catheter-injectable, self-healing hydrogels for localized and sustained drug delivery to the beating heart.

Learn more →

3D-Bioprinted Cardiovascular Tissue Models for Rapid Drug Screening

Creating printable, cell-supportive bioinks for rapid drug screening and cardiotoxicity testing in diverse populations.

Learn more →

In situ gelling polymers for abdominal aortic aneurysm therapy

Designing minimally invasive biomaterials that rapidly gel in the body to stabilize aneurysms and promote vascular regeneration.
